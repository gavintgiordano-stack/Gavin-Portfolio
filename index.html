Gavin Giordano

Ceramics Portfolio

My ceramics work is centered on learning to let go of expectations. Every piece begins the same way: a lump of clay placed on the wheel, yet no two pieces ever end the same. Like snowflakes forming from the same water but emerging uniquely, each vessel develops its own character through subtle changes in pressure, movement, glaze, and firing. The process has taught me to embrace uncertainty and appreciate the beauty of imperfection. By leaning into small variations and imperfections rather than correcting them, I create pieces that are uniquely their own. These individual details come together to form a finished work that is richer, more personal, and impossible to replicate exactly.

Freshman Year

Developing basic wheel-throwing skills through simple functional forms, with attention to balance, proportion, and trimming.

Sophomore Year

Refining ceramic forms through cleaner finishing, stronger surface choices, and more intentional details, while also exploring lidded vessels as a way to expand construction and design skills.

Junior Year

Exploring more expressive ceramic forms through glaze experimentation, detailed trimming, and stronger surface choices, while also developing ribbed vessels as a way to emphasize texture and movement.
